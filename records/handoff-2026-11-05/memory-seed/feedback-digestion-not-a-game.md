The OAW digestion prototype is not a game. It exists to fix the best process for adding skills to OAW from now on, written as the official process, and to give users a digestion animation that both explains that process and lets them operate it (each food moves through the stages; the current stage's actions sit on the animation).

**Why:** on 2026-11-03 the user corrected "game" framing: "這不是遊戲 而是之後要開始增加skills時候最佳流程定出來 消化系統動畫讓使用者容易懂也能幫忙操作". Their earlier "像電動一樣簡單好用" meant simple to use.

**How to apply:** do not name commands, pages, docs or progress items "game"/"play"; do not defer the animation as decoration, since it is the operating interface. Recorded as D19 in the digestion decision record. See [[oaw-catalog-intake-rules]].
