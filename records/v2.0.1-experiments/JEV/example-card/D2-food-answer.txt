Ticket 52 (`docs/reporting-note.md`) describes unwanted narration on routine jobs. The active guide, identical to `runs/guides/2026-10-16.md`, explains the behavior: reports before tool work and after every tool call, each with eight sections and at least two sentences per section. That creates a minimum of sixteen sentences per report, regardless of task size. The saved requests ask only for an output path and test result, or a one-sentence test result. Neither requests a progress narrative.

The changed component is the mandatory progress-report block as a whole: observed effect **helped** for these two routine tasks; recommend **keep the deletion**, subject to user acceptance. The trials remove all three lines together, so they do not isolate each line's independent contribution. The remaining communication rules already cover the team's requested final result and verification. Today's host also supplies progress-update behavior; deleting local duplication lets applicable host and user instructions govern it. It does not require silence or promise zero progress messages on the current host.

Reject the ticket's sentence-cap suggestion for this proposal: it retains compulsory per-tool reporting and could conflict with the sixteen-sentence minimum unless other rules change too. Reject a separate reporting policy and template: the existing trial achieves the desired observed result by deletion, without another file, routing step, or format to maintain. Neither alternative has saved trial evidence; no behavioral benefit is claimed for them.
