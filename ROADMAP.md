# OAW roadmap

The active work is the [v2.0.0 execution plan](docs/plans/v2.0.0-execution.md).
The proposed release makes OAW native-first, fixes data integrity, and adds
safe migration and optional evidence-driven improvements.

After independent review, priorities are representative native-only ablations
on Claude Opus 5.5 and Codex GPT-6.1 Sol, then improvements justified by those
results. Additional runtimes, vector stores, background curators, and durable
workflow orchestration are not default dependencies.

Completed v1 work and experiments remain in CHANGELOG.md and docs/archive/.
